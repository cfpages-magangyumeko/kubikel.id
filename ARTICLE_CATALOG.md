# Article Catalog — Kubikel.id

## Scope and domain boundary

This catalog builds a non-geographic Indonesian knowledge system for office cubicles, toilet cubicle partitions, and electrical switchgear cubicles. It separates those systems before selection, links neutral education to verified commercial routes only when appropriate, and excludes city/province variants, invented prices, unsafe electrical procedures, unsupported compliance claims, and fabricated project evidence. Other owned cubicle domains may independently cover the same subjects.

## Catalog statistics

- 18 parent topics and 108 article briefs; six distinct briefs per topic.
- 12 P0 first-wave assets, followed by bounded design, delivery, and specialist-reference waves.
- Existing commercial sections retain verified transactional intent; articles own one informational task each.

## Priority legend

- `P0`: first bounded triage and safe-brief cluster.
- `P1`: core system and lifecycle support.
- `P2`: specialist reference requiring stronger evidence or expert review.
- `P3`: reserved; no current brief depends on low-value filler.
